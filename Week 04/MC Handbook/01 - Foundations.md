
# Foundations

## What is Monte Carlo?
Monte Carlo simulation estimates the distribution of possible outcomes by repeatedly
sampling uncertain inputs and evaluating a deterministic model.

## History
- Developed during the Manhattan Project.
- Key contributors: Stanislaw Ulam and John von Neumann.
- Named after the Monte Carlo casino because of its reliance on chance.

## Why it Works
- Law of Large Numbers
- Central Limit Theorem
- Random sampling approximates expected behavior.

## Deterministic vs Probabilistic Thinking
Deterministic: one answer.
Probabilistic: distribution of answers.

## Common Distributions
- Uniform
- Normal
- Lognormal
- Triangular
- Exponential
- Weibull
- Beta

## Thought Process
Identify uncertainty first, not equations first.
