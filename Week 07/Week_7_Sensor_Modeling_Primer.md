# Week 7 Primer — Sensor Modeling

## Purpose
Understand how real sensors differ from ideal measurements.

## Topics
- GPS
- IMUs
- Accelerometers
- Gyroscopes
- Magnetometers
- Bias
- Scale factor
- White noise
- Bias random walk
- Timing latency

## Aerospace Applications
- Flight computers
- Satellite navigation
- Autonomous drones

## Key Takeaways
- Every sensor contains uncertainty.
- Good models improve estimator performance.
- Noise characteristics matter as much as accuracy.

## Exercises
- Simulate increasing bias drift.
- Compare low-noise and high-noise IMUs.
