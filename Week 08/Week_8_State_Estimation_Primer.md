# Week 8 Primer — State Estimation

## Purpose
Estimate quantities that cannot be measured directly by combining models with noisy measurements.

## Topics
- Bayesian estimation
- Gaussian distributions
- Covariance
- Kalman Filter
- Extended Kalman Filter
- Unscented Kalman Filter
- Innovation
- Sensor fusion

## Aerospace Applications
- GPS/INS integration
- Spacecraft attitude estimation
- Autonomous vehicle localization

## Key Takeaways
- Filters balance prediction and measurement.
- Covariance represents uncertainty.
- Proper tuning of Q and R is essential.

## Exercises
- Tune Q and R.
- Remove GPS updates and observe drift.
