# Week 6 Primer — Guidance

## Purpose
Learn how vehicles decide where to go before controllers decide how to get there.

## Topics
- Guidance vs Navigation vs Control
- Coordinate frames
- Waypoint following
- Pure Pursuit
- Line-of-Sight guidance
- Proportional Navigation
- Dubins paths
- Trajectory generation

## Aerospace Applications
- UAV waypoint missions
- Missile interception
- Autonomous ground vehicles
- Planetary rovers

## Key Takeaways
- Guidance generates desired trajectories.
- Navigation estimates current state.
- Control follows guidance commands.

## Exercises
- Compare Pure Pursuit and Proportional Navigation.
- Modify waypoint spacing and observe tracking.
